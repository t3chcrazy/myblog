## Design Specificity
Clearly authored for this product (engraved halftone banners, masthead ears, two-column lead with drop cap, numbered edition list, end-of-dispatch mark). The risk is too much costume: about a dozen invented labels (Index Telegraph, Printing Stamp, Certified Archival Ink, Concluded on the Front Page) make the world feel dressed up rather than lived in.
Detector: CLI scan of app/ and components/ clean. In-page detector (headless, 5 pages): no h1 on /blog, h1 to h3 skip on /blog/categories/mobile, code comment contrast 3.47:1, kicker-above-heading x5, cream-palette, overused-font, em-dash-overuse, monotonous-spacing. Kicker, palette, font and em-dash flags are likely false positives against the deliberate newspaper brief.

## Priority Issues
1. [P1] Wording contradicts the human-review promise: "Set, proofed & printed by an AI" (Masthead.tsx:64), "Published without a press, by an AI" (Footer.tsx:70), Colophon "written and typeset by an AI"; posts carry no disclosure. Fix: per-post note under the byline ("Researched by AI, reviewed and published by Abhishek Prashant", optionally PR link and sources); reword the masthead ear, footer line and Colophon. Command: clarify.
2. [P1] Accessibility basics: no h1 on the front page; "§" heading anchor enters heading accessible names; category page h1 to h3 skip; theme toggle lacks aria-pressed and its hidden label is wider than the button on mobile; mobile tap targets 13-22px (nav, tags, rail links); code comment contrast 3.47:1. Command: harden, adapt.
3. [P1] Front page repeats itself: lead story shown three times (lead, Wire, Desk 04); Wire and In This Edition both list the issue; desk counts duplicated; search row labelled twice; about 15 entry points above the desks. Fix: fold the Wire into In This Edition or make it "since last week"; show an earlier post in Desk 04; one search label; drop or repurpose the Printing Stamp. Command: distill.
4. [P2] Reading texture: justified sans body on desktop gives uneven word spacing; hyphens:auto breaks 4 words in 6 lines on the mobile lead. Fix: ragged-right body, hyphenate-limit-chars 7 4 4 or no hyphenation under 640px; consider Newsreader body at about 20px. Command: typeset.
5. [P2] Empty and missing states read as holes: empty Mobile desk tile, grey placeholder for the bannerless Tailwind card. Fix: "space reserved" treatment for the empty desk; text-only card when a post has no banner. Command: delight.

## Persona Red Flags
- Jordan (first-timer): no plain one-liner saying what Auxesis is and how it is made; "Index Telegraph" and "The Wire" are opaque; "No. 5" could mean issue or post.
- Sam (screen reader / keyboard): no h1 on the front page; headings announced with "§"; theme toggle has no on/off state. Skip link and focus outlines are good.
- Casey (mobile, one-handed): nav links 22px tall in two centred rows with an orphan row; tag links 13px; lead excerpt runs about 500px before "Continued inside"; no quick reach to search or the next post.
- Priya (senior engineer, Saturday skim): no "one idea / apply this" box; read time easy to miss; no visible source list.

## Minor Observations
- Masthead date shows today, not the issue date.
- Archive is a flat list; grouping by issue or month would fit the format.
- Desk-grid image heights differ (200-210px), so headlines don't line up.
- 404 page has two headings with the same meaning.
- Next.js suggests eager loading for the lead banner (LCP image).
- Nav bullet separators at 1.37:1 contrast; fine if decorative, but should be hidden from screen readers.
